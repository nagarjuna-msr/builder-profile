The distribution bet

His current focus, and the conviction behind it.

His bet: as agents turn content into a default surface for companies and individuals, distribution becomes the bottleneck, and demand for cracking it grows fast.

Distribution does not get solved by posting more. It gets solved by a content-intelligence system where agents surface the real opportunities and a human applies taste, judgment, and original insight. That system is what he is building, and it is what most of the work below already does: the value funnel, the mechanism decoder, the virality study, the cracked revenue model, the sealed retrodiction harness.

He is dogfooding the thesis on his own channels (@stockvid.telugu on Instagram and YouTube). The current run: grow from about 3,500 followers toward 30,000+ total, roughly 20,000 on Instagram and 10,000 on YouTube, within about a month, driven by these systems. That figure is the target he is building toward, not a current count.

The early signal, and the one hard number here: a single reel surfaced by these insights reached about 164,000 views (API-confirmed).
